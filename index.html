About

Who I am

I'm a backend engineer who finds the interesting problems at the intersection of scale and correctness — where a naive solution breaks in production and a well-designed one runs quietly for years. I care deeply about writing maintainable, testable code and building systems whose failure modes you can reason about.

My career started with internships at EY and Samsung, where I got early exposure to enterprise-grade development and cross-functional delivery. That foundation shaped how I think about engineering — not just correctness, but reliability, observability, and team-level ownership.

At Trukker, I've spent 4+ years building the backend powering freight operations across the MENA region — from API design and Kafka pipelines to cloud deployments on AWS. Currently open to senior backend or platform engineering roles where the technical bar is high and the impact is tangible.

My dual degree in M.Sc. Mathematics and B.E. Electronics & Communication from BITS Pilani still shapes how I approach problems — with a preference for first principles over cargo-culted patterns.
